# 🌸 Floret

> *A free garden game for discovering the flowers of the world.*

**Status: 🚧 In progress.** Floret is under active development. Some features below are built, others are planned. See the [Roadmap](#roadmap).

---

## The Story Behind Floret

Floret began with a simple moment.

One afternoon at university, the author was sitting with two of her friends when they noticed a flower nearby. It was beautiful, and none of them knew what it was called. They searched, guessed, and argued about it, and the name stayed a mystery.

That small question stuck: *why is it so hard to learn the name of something you can see right in front of you?*

The first idea was a camera that could scan a flower and tell you its name and story, which is where the name **Floret Scans** came from. As the idea grew, it changed shape. Instead of a tool you point at a flower, it became a place you can *play* in: a game where discovering flowers is the fun part, and learning their names, origins and meanings happens naturally along the way.

Floret is the result: a cozy, free-to-play garden game where curiosity is the main reward.

---

## What Is Floret?

Floret is a garden-building game inspired by the *Grow a Garden* style of play, but completely free and focused on learning. You pick a home, design a garden, plant real flowers, and care for animal companions. Every time you plant something, a short learning menu pops up so you walk away knowing something new.

---

## Features

### 🏡 Houses and Gardens
- Choose from multiple houses and switch between them at any time
- Select a garden type for each house
- Each location has its own look and feel

### 🌷 A Growing Flower Library
- A dedicated flowers menu with **50+ real flowers**, with the goal of covering as many known flowers as possible over time
- Each flower has its own entry: name, family, origin, colours, and fun facts

### 📖 Learn As You Plant
- Planting a flower opens a pop-up with information about it
- Designed to help learners remember names and details through play, not memorisation

### 🐾 Animal Companions
- Choose from **10+ animals**
- Pet your companion
- Feed it using food from your **backpack menu**
- Your animal follows you around the garden

### 🎨 Look and Feel
- Retro, **Mario Kart-style 2D main menu** with pink **PLAY** and **EXIT** options
- A calm, sophisticated front page with a soft, modern animated background (text stays still)
- Rich colours and realistic visuals, with shaders planned for a high-definition look

### 🆓 Free to Play
- No coins and no paywalls for now. The focus is exploring and learning.

## Future Development

Floret is a work in progress and can grow well beyond its current form. Possible directions include:

- **Unity**, **Godot**, or **Unreal Engine** for richer 3D visuals, better performance and more advanced UI
- **Machine learning** to bring back the original idea: scanning a real flower with a camera and identifying it, and potentially smarter, more polished interfaces
- More flowers, more animals, more locations, and more ways to learn
